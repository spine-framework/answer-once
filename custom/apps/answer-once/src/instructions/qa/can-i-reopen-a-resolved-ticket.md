## Q

Can I reopen a resolved ticket?

## A

Replying on a resolved ticket's thread is the right first step — the
conversation is still there. If the answer genuinely didn't hold or the problem
came back, open a new ticket and reference the old one; that keeps the history
and gives triage a fresh shot at it (and flags the published answer for review
if it turned out to be wrong).
