## Q

What do the ticket statuses mean?

## A

The statuses you'll see on your tickets:

- **AI responding** — triage is checking the knowledge base for an answer.
- **Pending resolution** — the AI posted an answer it's confident about. If it
  solved your problem, you don't need to do anything; the ticket auto-resolves.
- **Human assigned / In progress** — a support operator owns your ticket.
- **Resolved** — your question was answered and the ticket is closed out.
- **Closed** — fully finished; no further action expected.
