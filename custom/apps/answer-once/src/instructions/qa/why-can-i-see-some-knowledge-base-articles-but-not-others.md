## Q

Why can I see some knowledge base articles but not others?

## A

Articles have a learning scope. **Private** articles stay inside the
account they were earned in. **Internal** articles are visible to support
staff. **Shared** articles are published platform-wide and are visible to every
customer account. You see your own account's articles plus everything shared —
you never see another customer's private knowledge.
