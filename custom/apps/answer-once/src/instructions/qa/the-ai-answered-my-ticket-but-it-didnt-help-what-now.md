## Q

The AI answered my ticket but it didn't help — what now?

## A

Just reply on the ticket thread saying what's still wrong or what's
missing. An unsatisfied or contested AI answer escalates to a human support
operator — you don't need to open a new ticket. Your reply is also what teaches
the system that the existing answer wasn't good enough.
