## Q

Why did I get an answer instantly instead of waiting for support?

## A

Your question matched a published knowledge article — someone asked
something equivalent before, a human resolved it, and the resolution was
reviewed and published. The answer you received is that article, cited so you
can read the full version. If it's not quite right, reply and it will go to a
human.
