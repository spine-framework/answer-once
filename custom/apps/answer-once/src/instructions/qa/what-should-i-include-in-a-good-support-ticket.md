## Q

What should I include in a good support ticket?

## A

Three things make tickets resolve fastest: **what you were trying to
do**, **what actually happened** (exact error text helps), and **what you
expected instead**. Include the page or feature you were using. You don't need
to know the internal names — describe it in your own words and triage will do
the matching.
