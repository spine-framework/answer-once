## Q

How does an answer get into the knowledge base?

## A

Through real support work. A ticket gets resolved, a case-analysis pass
drafts a candidate article from the resolution, and a support reviewer checks
it — correcting content, choosing a learning scope — before publishing. Nothing
publishes automatically without review, which is why the KB stays trustworthy
enough to auto-answer with.
