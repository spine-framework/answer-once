## Q

How do I submit a support ticket?

## A

From your portal, go to **Tickets → New Ticket** (or ask from the
dashboard). Give it a clear title and describe what you're trying to do, what
happened, and what you expected. As soon as you submit, our triage checks the
published knowledge base — if there's a matching answer you'll see it right
away.
