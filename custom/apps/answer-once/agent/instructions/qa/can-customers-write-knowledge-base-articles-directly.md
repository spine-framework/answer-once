## Q

Can customers write knowledge base articles directly?

## A

No — articles are earned from resolved tickets and published only after
staff review. If you have a tip or correction for an existing article, open a
ticket about it; that ticket becomes the input for a reviewed article update.
This is intentional: unreviewed knowledge would poison the auto-answering.
