## Q

Will I get an email when support replies?

## A

If the deployment has email configured, yes — a staff reply on your
ticket sends you an email notification in addition to the in-app notification.
Either way, the full conversation is always on the ticket page. (On sites
without email configured, watch the ticket or check back in the portal.)
