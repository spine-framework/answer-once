## Q

What is the Knowledgebase page?

## A

**Knowledgebase** is the library of published answers — articles that
came out of real, resolved support work and passed review. You can browse and
search it directly; it's the same corpus the AI cites when it answers tickets.
If an article answers your question, you never had to wait for a ticket at all.
