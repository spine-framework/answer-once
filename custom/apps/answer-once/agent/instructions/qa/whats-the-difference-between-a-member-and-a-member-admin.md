## Q

What's the difference between a member and a member_admin?

## A

Both are roles on your customer account. A **member** can open tickets,
reply, watch tickets, and browse the knowledge base. A **member_admin** does
all of that plus account administration — inviting and managing teammates.
Support staff roles (`support`, `support_admin`) are separate and only exist on
the company's side.
