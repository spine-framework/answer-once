## Q

What happens after I submit a ticket?

## A

Your ticket is triaged automatically first. If a published knowledge
article answers it, you get that answer immediately with a citation. If not,
the ticket is assigned to our support team and a human replies in the ticket
thread. Either way, everything happens in the same thread on your ticket page —
you can watch progress from **Tickets**.
