## Q

Does Answer Once learn from my tickets?

## A

Yes — that's the point of the product, and it's done deliberately. When
your ticket is resolved, a case-analysis pass can turn the resolution into a
candidate knowledge article, which a human reviews before anything publishes.
What publishes is up to the reviewer: it can stay **private** to your account,
go **internal** for support staff, or be **shared** platform-wide in sanitized
form. Your raw tickets never become someone else's article directly — only the
reviewed, generalized lesson does.
