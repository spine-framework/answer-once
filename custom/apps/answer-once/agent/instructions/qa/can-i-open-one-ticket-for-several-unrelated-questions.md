## Q

Can I open one ticket for several unrelated questions?

## A

You can, but one question per ticket works much better. Each resolved
ticket potentially becomes one knowledge article — a bundled ticket produces a
muddier answer and a worse KB entry. Separate tickets also triage
independently, so the known half answers instantly instead of waiting on the
unknown half.
