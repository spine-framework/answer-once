## Q

Can I browse the knowledge base without opening a ticket?

## A

Yes — the **Knowledgebase** page is open for browsing and searching
anytime. It's usually the fastest path: if the answer exists, you have it in
seconds. Tickets are for what the KB doesn't know yet.
